Popular Destinations

Handpicked iconic cities and scenic landscapes that capture the true essence of India.

City of Dreams

Mumbai

India's financial capital featuring historic colonial architecture, bustling markets, the Arabian Sea promenade, and legendary Bollywood cinema.

Beach Haven

Goa

A tropical paradise famed for sunny palm-fringed beaches, centuries-old Portuguese churches, tranquil spice estates, and exciting water sports.

Mountain Retreat

Manali

A breathtaking hill station nestled in Himachal Pradesh, offering snow-capped Himalayan peaks, pine forests, hot springs, and outdoor adventures.

Paradise on Earth

Kashmir

Known for tranquil wooden houseboats floating on Dal Lake, scenic Mughal gardens, verdant valleys, and world-renowned alpine meadows.

The Pink City

Jaipur

The regal capital of Rajasthan showcasing majestic Rajput hill forts, glorious terracotta palaces, royal museums, and vibrant colorful bazaars.

God's Own Country

Kerala

A serene coastal wonderland celebrated for emerald backwater houseboats, rolling mist-covered tea plantations, and relaxing Ayurvedic wellness.

Helpful Advice

Travel Tips

Essential guidelines to ensure a safe, smooth, and enjoyable trip across India.

☀️

Check Weather

Check the weather before travelling to pack suitable clothing for tropical plains or cold mountains.

📄

Essential Documents

Carry important documents like government photo IDs, permits, hotel vouchers, and keep digital copies.

📞

Emergency Contacts

Keep emergency contacts handy including local police (112), medical helplines, and family numbers.

🙏

Respect Local Culture

Respect local culture, traditions, and dress codes when visiting sacred temples, monuments, and rural villages.

🌱

Keep Clean

Keep your surroundings clean by disposing of trash responsibly and avoiding single-use plastic waste.
